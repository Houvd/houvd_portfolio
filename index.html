Music as expression, collaboration and shared experience.

I experience music as the deepest form of my artistic expression and as a space for connection, sharing and collaboration.

Through songwriting, music production, collective projects and cultural events, I aim to create artistic experiences that connect personal expression, shared emotions and creative exchange.

I am especially drawn to collaborations with artists and people who are able to communicate something authentic through their sensitivity, vision and artistic language.

Artist Bio

Giuseppe Longo, alias Houvd, is a Calabrian artist born in 2001 at Vibo Valentia. Growing up in close contact with music, he developed a strong passion for hip hop culture from a young age, combining his study of drums with the listening of Italian and international rap, R&B and reggae artists.

In 2020, he began releasing self-produced songs, developing over the years a personal path based on songwriting, production and sound experimentation. In 2022, he released the EP Voce, a project that represents an important step in his artistic research.

Alongside his creative work, he pursued technical studies related to music informatics and audio technologies, approaching music also from an engineering perspective. Today, he continues to produce, write and collaborate, experiencing music as a form of personal expression and a space for sharing.

Collective Founder

Twin H Collective

Twin H Collective is an artistic collective focused mainly on music, born with the aim of creating and promoting collaborative projects rooted in sound, identity and cultural expression.

While music represents its starting point, the collective's long-term vision is to expand into audiovisual content, cultural events and multidisciplinary artistic experiences.

As a founder and active member, I contribute to the artistic direction, music production, project organization and development of the collective's creative identity.

Cultural Event Co-organizer

Rinascita Flow

Produced by Altrove Associazione Culturale
Co-organized with Twin H Collective & High Monkey Studio

A cultural event dedicated to the promotion of Hip Hop culture in the Vibo Valentia area. The project was created to give space to local artistic voices and strengthen the connection between music, territory and community.

The event celebrates Hip Hop as a cultural language based on expression, identity, sharing and social presence, creating opportunities for artists and audiences to connect.

My role — co-organization, creative and technical support, artistic direction and operational coordination.

I also explore music as a narrative and emotional device for visual and stage-based projects. In these works, sound becomes a way to support the atmosphere, enhance the storytelling and connect the audience with the emotional identity of the piece.

Short Film · 2024

Il Gioco delle Sedie

Set inside an anonymous facility where individuals live under a rigid and repetitive daily ritual. Every day at 3:00 PM, the routine culminates in the "Gioco delle Sedie" — musical chairs — turning a childhood game into a symbolic struggle for power, recognition and survival.

Director Anastasia Vittoria Sarro
Production Studio Ireos Student Team
Funded by Politecnico di Torino
Original music & programming Emilio Bazan
Co-composition & arrangement Ariadna Ortega
My role — Author of the ballad "Il Gioco delle Sedie"
